4+ Years at the Wheel

The Maker Behind the Clay

Hi, I'm John Ruiz — Dallas Pottery Artist

Four years ago I put my hands on a wheel and everything changed. Clay is an ancient teacher — it slows you down, pulls you into the present, and gives something back every time you show up for it.

Spirit Tree Ceramics is a collection of handmade functional art and fine stoneware fired with intention in my Dallas studio, designed to bring the grounding essence of raw clay into your everyday life.

  • 🏺
    One-of-a-Kind Pieces Every pot in the shop is handthrown and glazed individually in my studio. Own a piece of the slow craft.
  • 🔥
    Stoneware & Porcelain Clays are formulated for high-fire durability, ensuring each vase, bowl, and mug is fully functional.
  • 🌵
    Rooted in Texas Crafted locally in Dallas, TX. Each piece carries the distinct grounding energy of raw earth.
